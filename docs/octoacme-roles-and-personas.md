# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, technical strategy, and design reviews. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Establish technical design approach and technology choices
- Review architecture decisions and code quality patterns
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide integration points and cross-system dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve system reliability
- Build team technical capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and design recommendations
- Technical risk assessments in project planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on design decisions and best practices
- Advises Product Managers on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- Works with Security Lead on architecture security implications
- Partners with QA/Testing Lead on testability and quality approaches

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy and testing processes. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and maintain high quality standards throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plan for the project
- Design and execute test cases for features and integrations
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Establish quality metrics and acceptance criteria validation
- Advocate for testability in design and implementation
- Mentor team members on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality and acceptance standards
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- QA sign-off on acceptance criteria during planning
- Test results and defect reports
- Collaboration with developers on testability
- Quality metrics in weekly status updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers to ensure code quality and testability
- Works with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria
- Collaborates with Technical Lead on test strategy and coverage
- Coordinates with Operations/DevOps on deployment verification

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure projects meet compliance requirements and implement security best practices throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and compliance needs
- Conduct security assessments and threat modeling
- Review code and infrastructure for security vulnerabilities
- Guide secure development practices and incident response
- Maintain audit trails and compliance documentation
- Educate team on security principles and standards

### Goals
- Minimize security risks and vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory requirements
- Build security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning
- Security testing and review sign-offs
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Security training and awareness sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews technical designs with Technical Lead/Architect
- Advises Developers on secure coding practices
- Partners with Operations/DevOps on infrastructure security
- Supports Project Manager on risk register and escalation paths

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide business context, resource allocation authority, and strategic alignment for projects. They are the ultimate decision-makers for go/no-go decisions and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Allocate budget and resources
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Address escalations and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate project value to leadership
- Approve major scope or timeline changes

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivery
- Ensure projects align with organizational strategy
- Manage risk at the enterprise level

### Typical Communication
- Milestone approval meetings and steering committee updates
- Risk escalations and decision gate meetings
- Executive status reports and business case reviews
- Resource allocation and conflict resolution

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Project Manager on governance and escalation paths
- Partners with Product Manager on strategic priorities and outcomes
- Addresses blockers across engineering, operations, and other teams
- Provides final approval for releases and major decisions

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and production readiness. They ensure reliable, scalable systems and smooth releases to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD systems
- Manage infrastructure, monitoring, and alerting
- Ensure production readiness and incident response capabilities
- Plan rollback procedures and disaster recovery
- Optimize system performance and reliability
- Document operational runbooks and procedures

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, secure, and scalable infrastructure
- Reduce deployment risk and cycle time
- Minimize production incidents and downtime

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Infrastructure and capacity planning reviews
- Production incidents and post-mortems
- Operational metrics and dashboards

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers on deployment and instrumentation practices
- Collaborates with Security Lead on infrastructure security
- Partners with Project Manager on deployment scheduling
- Advises Product Manager on capacity and scale considerations

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical implementation. They clarify user needs, validate requirements, and ensure solutions address business problems effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into technical requirements
- Validate solution design against business goals
- Create requirements documentation and user stories
- Facilitate communication between business and technical teams
- Conduct user acceptance testing (UAT)

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address actual business problems
- Reduce rework due to misunderstood requirements
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions and workshops
- User story refinement and acceptance criteria definition
- Stakeholder validation and sign-off
- UAT coordination and results reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager on requirements and prioritization
- Works with Project Manager on scope and stakeholder communication
- Partners with Developers on requirement clarification
- Supports QA/Testing Lead on acceptance criteria validation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile delivery processes and remove impediments to team progress. They coach the team on agile practices and help maintain sustainable delivery pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and mindsets
- Maintain sprint board and tracking metrics
- Support continuous improvement and process optimization
- Shield team from external distractions during sprints

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, predictable delivery
- Improve team velocity and collaboration
- Foster continuous improvement culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Retrospective notes and action items
- Process metrics and team feedback

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Project Manager on scheduling and milestone planning
- Supports team members across all roles in process adherence
- Escalates impediments to Product Manager and Project Manager
- Partners with managers on team health and morale
